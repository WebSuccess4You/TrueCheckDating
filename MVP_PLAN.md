# TrueCheck.ai MVP Plan

## Business Question

The MVP must answer:

> Will adults use and pay for a private, understandable review of online dating warning signs before sending money, traveling, sharing sensitive information, or making major commitments?

## Product Promise

TrueCheck.ai helps users organize evidence, identify recognized warning patterns, find inconsistencies, and choose safer next steps.

It does not:

- declare someone a criminal
- guarantee that someone is genuine
- confirm a legal identity
- replace law enforcement, legal counsel, financial advice, or professional investigation
- secretly monitor or record another person

## Included in the First Sellable Version

### Public Experience

- Landing page
- How It Works
- Pricing
- Privacy summary
- Terms and disclaimer links
- Registration
- Login
- Password reset

### Private User Experience

- Dashboard
- Create Case
- Saved Cases
- AI Chat Analyzer
- Profile Consistency Check
- Video Call Verifier
- Guided Reverse Image Checker
- Combined Final Risk Report
- Printable or downloadable report
- Account settings
- Case deletion
- Account deletion

### Revenue Features

- Free preliminary screening
- One-time paid full report
- Monthly membership
- Stripe checkout and billing management
- Payment confirmation and failed-payment handling

### Administration

- Aggregate account, case, and analysis totals
- Technical failure review
- Prompt-version and application-version display
- User support lookup with restricted access
- No casual browsing of private user content

## Explicitly Deferred

- Native iPhone and Android applications
- Facial recognition
- Automatic criminal or financial background searches
- Internet-wide automated reverse-image crawling
- Social-media scraping
- Hidden audio or video recording
- Live biometric analysis
- Public scammer lists
- Community accusations
- Location tracking
- Complex affiliate marketplace
- Enterprise organization accounts

## Definition of MVP Completion

A new user must be able to:

1. Register and verify an account
2. Create a private case
3. Paste a conversation and receive a preliminary analysis
4. Complete the profile questionnaire
5. Record reverse-image search findings
6. Complete the video-call verification checklist
7. Purchase or unlock the full report
8. View the combined report
9. Print or download the report
10. Return later and reopen the case
11. Delete the case
12. Delete the account and associated data

The MVP is not complete until the Build 14 integrated checkpoint and later security, staging, evaluation, and launch gates pass.

## Build 14 Checkpoint

The coded product now includes administration and support. Live Supabase, OpenAI, Stripe, privacy, export, deletion, and staff-role testing must be completed before security hardening begins.
