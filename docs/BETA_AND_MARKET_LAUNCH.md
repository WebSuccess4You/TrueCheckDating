# Beta and Market Launch

## 1. Private Beta Goal

Determine whether real users:

- understand the product
- can complete the workflow
- trust the explanations without mistaking them for proof
- find the report useful
- will pay for the complete result

## 2. Beta Size

Approximately 20 to 50 invited adults.

Recruit a mix of:

- online dating users
- people who have experienced suspicious relationship behavior
- people with legitimate long-distance relationships
- nontechnical phone users
- users of different ages

Avoid using nonconsensual intimate material.

## 3. Beta Instructions

Testers should preferably use:

- fictional cases
- redacted conversations
- their own conversations
- material they have a lawful right to submit

## 4. Beta Metrics

Track:

- sign-up completion
- case creation
- chat submission completion
- profile-check completion
- image-check completion
- video-check completion
- report purchase intent
- actual test purchase completion
- report download
- abandonment point
- support requests
- time to first result
- AI failure rate
- average cost per case

## 5. Beta Questions

Ask:

- What decision were you trying to make?
- Did the report help you make a safer decision?
- Which section was most useful?
- Which wording was confusing?
- Did you understand that the score was not proof?
- What information did you hesitate to provide?
- Would you pay $9.99 for the full report?
- Would you pay $14.99 per month?
- What would make you trust the product more?

## 6. Beta Exit Criteria

- most testers complete the core flow without direct help
- no unresolved critical security defect
- no recurring payment or entitlement failure
- users understand the limitations
- serious hallucination and unsupported-claim rates meet the evaluation threshold
- at least some users demonstrate genuine willingness to pay
- support workload is manageable

## 7. Public Positioning

Preferred:

> Review conversations, profile claims, image-search findings, and verification behavior for warning signs before sending money or making major commitments.

Avoid:

> Prove whether your online partner is a scammer.

## 8. Initial Marketing Channels

- TikTok
- YouTube Shorts
- Instagram Reels
- educational articles
- email follow-up
- online-dating safety content
- partnerships with relationship and scam-awareness educators where permitted

## 9. Initial Content Themes

- excuses used to avoid video calls
- warning signs before sending money
- how to verify a long-distance relationship safely
- why a photo alone proves little
- how emergencies create pressure
- how to preserve evidence privately
- what a risk score can and cannot tell you
- how to ask for safe verification without confrontation

## 10. Launch Stages

### Stage A — Internal

Owner and development testing.

### Stage B — Private Alpha

Small trusted group using synthetic cases.

### Stage C — Private Beta

20 to 50 invited testers.

### Stage D — Soft Public Launch

Limited organic promotion and monitored paid transactions.

### Stage E — Broader Marketing

Only after conversion, support, cost, and safety metrics are understood.

## 11. Support

Before public launch:

- support email
- response templates
- billing issue process
- deletion request process
- safety escalation wording
- abuse-reporting process

## 12. Post-Launch Review

Review weekly at first:

- defects
- AI quality
- payment conversion
- refunds
- user confusion
- cost per analysis
- abuse attempts
- feature requests

Do not add major features until the core product demonstrates value.
