# TrueCheck.ai Owner Decisions

Approved by William H. Stewart on June 17, 2026.

## 1. Product Promise

Approved:

TrueCheck.ai helps people identify warning signs, inconsistencies, and risky behavior in online dating relationships before they send money, travel, or make major commitments.

It does not claim to prove that someone is a scammer, criminal, genuine, or safe.

## 2. First Target Customer

Approved:

Adults communicating with a romantic interest online who are uncertain whether the person is genuine, truthful, or financially manipulative.

## 3. First Release Format

Approved:

Launch first as a mobile-friendly website that works on phones, tablets, and computers.

Do not begin with an Apple App Store or Google Play application.

## 4. First Sellable Features

Approved:

- user registration and login
- private saved cases
- AI Chat Analyzer
- Profile Consistency Check
- Video Call Verifier
- Guided Reverse Image Checker
- Combined Final Risk Report
- printable or downloadable report
- account and case deletion

The first version guides the user through external reverse-image searching and records findings. Automated internet-wide image searching is deferred.

## 5. Payment Method

Approved:

- one-time payment for an individual full report
- monthly membership for multiple cases or repeated analyses

## 6. Free Offer

Approved:

A free preliminary screening shows limited findings. Payment unlocks the complete report, detailed recommendations, and downloadable version.

## 7. Initial Pricing Direction

Approved for testing:

- individual full report: approximately $9.99
- monthly membership: approximately $14.99

## 8. Report Language

Approved concern labels:

- Low
- Moderate
- High
- Critical

Reports also display:

- risk score
- confidence
- evidence completeness
- warning signs
- protective signals
- recommended next steps
- limitations

## 9. Privacy

Approved:

- cases are private to the account owner
- users can delete cases
- users can delete their accounts
- private conversations are not publicly searchable
- submitted conversations and photographs are not sold
- submitted material is retained only as long as needed to provide the service and meet limited operational requirements

## 10. Release Plan

Approved:

Begin with a private beta of approximately 20 to 50 invited testers. Correct problems before opening the website to paying customers and broader promotion.
